When the game is over, **Finish this game** brings everything together on one screen: the result, the squad and goals, the match sheet and the match report.

## Opening it

- At full time on the large clock: **Finish this game**
- Any time from the match menu: **Finish this game**

The menu row shows the progress, e.g. **1/2**. The dot turns green when the match is complete.

## What "complete" means

A match is complete when:

1. **The squad is selected**, and
2. **The goal log matches the score** - as many goals are logged as the scoreboard shows.

Scorers are optional: a goal without a scorer still counts as logged. The match report, positions and other records do not affect the counter. The same rule applies to every game, old ones included.

## The screen from top to bottom

### 1. Result, squad and goals

- Result card: teams, score, date, venue and length (extra time and penalties too, if played)
- **Squad: 14 players** · **Change** - opens the squad for editing
- Goal list: tap a goal to edit its time, scorer or assister, or delete it. **Add a goal** adds a missing one.

### 2. Match sheet

The amber **Match sheet** button opens the match data ready to copy:

- the squad with numbers and full names; goalkeeper (GK) and captain (C) marked, and each player's position after the name (the recorded actual position, else the starting position)
- the score, half-time and extra time when played
- goals as minute, #number, name (assister)

Tap **Copy** and paste the text where you need it.

### 3. Match report

The match report is your own text about the match. There are three ways to make it:

- **Write** it yourself. **Template** adds ready headings (e.g. Overview, How the game unfolded, What went well, What we're working on, Next step).
- **Dictate the report** - say the report out loud and the text is added to it.
- **Draft with AI** - once you have connected your own AI provider (Settings → **Voice notes and AI**), the app drafts a report from the match data and your notes. You choose which parts are saved. Without a connection, the section explains how to connect one.

### 4. Share match report

**Share match report** below the report builds the text to send: the score, date and venue, scorers and assisters, the line-up by first name and your whole match report. You can edit the text before sending. **Share** opens the phone's share menu, **Copy** copies the text.

## Extra records

Below these are the extra records:

- **Checklist** - what has been recorded: Squad selected, Goals logged, Goal scorers named, Match report, Notes about players, Positions played, Player assessments (when on) and Competition & team (if you have leagues or tournaments). The list is a helper; the completeness counter looks only at the squad and the goals.
- **Notes** - observations dictated and written during the game
- **Positions played** - where each player actually played; shows in the player statistics
- **Player assessments** - if turned on in Settings (see [Roster Management](/guide/roster-management))
- **Export Excel** - the match data as an Excel file

At the bottom are the match staff and a folded **Player Stats** table.

<Callout type="tip">
The shortest routine: check the score and goals, copy the **Match sheet**, write or dictate a couple of sentences in the match report and tap **Share match report**.
</Callout>

## Later

Everything can be edited later. Open the game from Home (**Games** → **Saved games**) and choose **Finish this game** from the match menu.

Statistics that build up: [Statistics](/guide/statistics).
