When the game is over, **Finish this game** brings everything together on one screen: the result, the squad and goals, the match sheet and the match report.

## Opening it

- At full time on the large clock: **Finish this game**
- Any time from the match menu: **Finish this game**

The menu row shows the progress, e.g. **1/2**. The dot turns green when the match is complete.

## What "complete" means

A match is complete when:

1. **The squad is selected**, and
2. **The goals are logged** - **Match events** lists as many goals as the score.

Scorers are optional: a goal without a scorer still counts as logged. The match report, positions and other records do not affect the counter. The same rule applies to every game, old ones included.

## The screen from top to bottom

### 1. Result, squad and goals

- Result card: teams, score, date, venue and length (extra time and penalties too, if played)
- **Squad: 14 players** · **Change** - opens the squad for editing
- **Match events**: the three-dot menu on a goal's row lets you edit its time, scorer and assister (**Edit**) or delete it (**Delete**). An opponent goal can only be deleted. **Add a goal** adds a missing one.

### 2. Match sheet

The amber **Match sheet** button opens the match sheet. It has every detail you need to fill in the official match sheet in Taso:

- the squad with numbers and full names (a player without a number has "–" in its place); goalkeeper (GK) and captain (C) marked, and each player's position after the name (the recorded actual position, else the starting position)
- the score, half-time and extra time when played
- goals: minute, the scorer's number and name, and the assister's number and name (e.g. "12' 7 Onni Virtanen (assist 9 Eino Laine)")

**Copy** puts the details on the clipboard so you can look at them while you fill in the match sheet, for example in Taso. **Share** opens the phone's share menu.

If a player in the squad has no shirt number, a line under the button says so, e.g. "2 players are missing a shirt number – Taso needs the numbers". **Add numbers** opens the player list, where you add them.

### 3. Match report

The match report is your own text about the match. Write it yourself: tap the report's text box. **Template** adds ready headings (e.g. Overview, How the game unfolded, What went well, What we're working on, Next step).

### 4. Share match report

**Share match report** below the report builds the text to send: the score, date and venue, scorers and assisters, the line-up by first name and your whole match report. You can edit the text before sending. **Share** opens the phone's share menu, **Copy** copies the text.

## Extra records

Below these are the extra records. In the Basic view they are folded under **More records**.

- **Checklist** - what has been recorded: Squad selected, Goals logged, Goal scorers named, Match report, Notes about players, Positions played and Competition & team (if you have leagues or tournaments). The list is a helper; the completeness counter looks only at the squad and the goals.
- **Notes** - observations written about the match and the players. Add a new one under **Add a note**. Voice notes recorded during the game wait under **Voice notes to review**: tap **Play**, type what you said, check the player and tap **Save note** (or **Discard**). A recording is deleted from the phone once you have handled it, and after 30 days at the latest. While recordings are waiting, the checklist shows a row "N voice notes to review".
- **Positions played** - where each player actually played; shows in the player statistics
- **Export Excel** - the match data as an Excel file

At the bottom are the match staff and a folded **Player Stats** table.

<Callout type="tip">
The shortest routine: check the score and goals, fill in the official match sheet from the **Match sheet**, write a couple of sentences in the match report and tap **Share match report**.
</Callout>

## Later

Everything can be edited later. Open the game from Home (in the Basic view **All games**, in the Full view **Games** → **Saved games**) and choose **Finish this game** from the match menu.
