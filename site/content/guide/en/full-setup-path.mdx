<Callout type="info">
This guide is about the **full layout**. New accounts start in the basic layout, where the team and players are created during setup (see [Quick Start](/guide/getting-started)). Switch with gear → **Settings** → **Layout** → **Full**, or from the basic-layout Home with **All features** → **Switch to the full layout**. Nothing is removed.
</Callout>

In the full layout Home has four tabs, plus the gear at the top right. Move between tabs by tapping a tab at the bottom or by swiping sideways.

- **Games** - the next match (or the last one played) as a large card, new match, recent results, saved games and the Match planner
- **Club** - the club card: counts of players, teams and staff (tap one to open the list), the season record and top scorer, plus warm-up, rules and coaching materials
- **Competitions** - the season record and the form of the last five games, plus the **Leagues** and **Tournaments** rows
- **Stats** - wins-draws-losses and goal difference, the top scorers, and league, tournament, overall and player stats
 When you set up your data in the order below, a new match fills in from the team and statistics build up by player, team and competition.

## Why set things up

- **Faster game creation** - pick a team and its players and linked competition fill in automatically
- **Complete statistics** - matches are linked to players, teams, leagues and tournaments
- **Season comparisons work** - the club season (e.g. "24/25") is worked out from the dates

## Order

0. Club season
1. Players
2. Staff
3. Leagues and tournaments
4. Teams
5. A match from the team

## Step 0: Club season

Gear → **Settings** → **General** → **Season Start Date** (**New season starts**). The previous season ends automatically the day before. The club season groups leagues, tournaments and statistics (e.g. "24/25").

## Step 1: Players

**Club** → **Players** opens **All Players**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a player">
Tap **Add Player** and fill in the name (required), nickname (shown on the disc), jersey number and notes.
</Step>
<Step title="Goalkeeper">
Open the player's details and mark goalkeepers with **Goalkeeper**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The same player can belong to several teams. More: [Roster Management](/guide/roster-management).

## Step 2: Staff

**Club** → **Personnel** → **Add Personnel**: name, role (e.g. Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Goalkeeper Coach), phone, email, certifications and notes. Staff are picked for a match in the game form or in **Match details**.

## Step 3: Leagues and tournaments

The **Competitions** tab has **Leagues** and **Tournaments**.

- **League** - league games. Name, official league, season, location, age group, sport, gender, periods and minutes, start and end date. The period settings prefill the league's games.
- **Tournament** - cups and tournaments. Also **Competition levels** (several levels in one tournament) and **Player of Tournament**.

Write the name without the season tag ("Spring League", not "Spring League 24/25") - the club season is shown automatically. More: [Teams & Organization](/guide/teams-organization).

## Step 4: Teams

**Club** → **Teams** → **Add Team**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Details">
Team name, kit colour and **Team Context**: league or tournament, level, age group and game type.
</Step>
<Step title="Roster">
On the **Roster** tab, **Edit Roster** and pick the team's players.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Step 5: A match from the team

**Games** → **New Game** opens **Create New Match**. Pick the team: its players and linked competition fill in automatically. **Repeat last game** fills the form with the previous match's settings. Optionally choose **Prefill from plan (optional)** if you made a plan for the game in the Match planner. Finally, **Create Game**.

<Callout type="tip">
"Not played yet" is on by default for future dates and off for today and past dates. The game is marked as played automatically the first time its clock starts.
</Callout>

## Home's Games tab

- The **Next match** card: the plan (**Show rotation**, **Share**) and **Start the match** / **Mark who is away**
- **Continue** - a match under way
- **Saved games** and **Match planner**
- The season summary: wins, draws and losses, goal difference and top scorer
