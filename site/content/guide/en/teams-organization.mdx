<Callout type="info">
Leagues, tournaments, teams and staff are managed in the **full layout** (Home's **Club** and **Competitions** tabs). The basic layout has one team, whose player list opens from the Team row on Home. Switch layouts with gear → **Settings** → **Layout**.
</Callout>

## Club season

The app works out a club season (e.g. "24/25") for each league and tournament from its start date and shows it with the name.

- Set the start date: gear → **Settings** → **General** → **Season Start Date**
- The previous season ends automatically the day before
- Do this before creating competitions so the grouping is right from the start

## Competitions

The top of the **Competitions** tab shows the season record (wins-draws-losses, goals) and the form of the last five games. Below it, the **Leagues** and **Tournaments** rows open the two lists. Both have search, archiving and actions for each item (edit, archive, delete).

### Leagues

A league groups league games and their statistics. League details:

- **Name** and **Official league** (or another league's name)
- **Season**, **Start Date** and **End Date**
- **Location**, **Age Group**, sport (Football or Futsal) and **Gender**
- **Periods** and **Minutes** - prefilled into the league's games
- **Color**, **Badge** and **Notes**

### Tournaments

A tournament has the same basics as a league, plus:

- **Competition levels** - when one tournament is played at several levels. A team and a game can be linked to a level, and statistics can be filtered by level.
- **Player of Tournament** - the tournament's awarded player

### Linking a game to a competition

In the game form (**Create New Match**) or in **Match details**, pick a league or tournament. If you pick a team with a linked competition, it fills in automatically. Linked games show in league and tournament statistics.

## Teams

**Club** → **Teams**. A team is a group of players you coach. The same player can belong to several teams.

### Creating a team

1. **Add Team**
2. Enter the **Team Name** and pick the kit colour
3. **Team Context**: league or tournament, level, age group and game type (all optional)
4. **Roster** tab → **Edit Roster** → pick the players

When you create a match and pick the team, its players and linked competition fill in automatically.

### Team actions

From the team list: **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Archive** and **Delete**. Deleting a team does not delete its games: they stay saved without a team and can be handled under **Manage games from deleted teams**.

### Placements

When editing a team, **Tournament & League Placements** lets you record the final placing (1st-10th) in each competition. The top three show medal badges.

### Naming

Keep team and competition names short and without a year: "PePo Lila", "Spring League", "Helsinki Cup". The club season is added to the display automatically ("Spring League 24/25").

## Staff

**Club** → **Personnel** → **Add Personnel**.

- **Name** and **Role**: Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Goalkeeper Coach, Fitness Coach, Physiotherapist, Team Manager, Support Staff or Other
- **Phone** and **Email** (optional)
- **Certifications** and **Notes**

Staff are picked for a match in the game form or in **Match details**. They show in **Finish this game** and in the Excel export. Deleting a person also removes them from every match they were picked for.

## Other Club tab items

The top of the Club tab is the club card: counts of players, teams and staff (tap a count to open the list), the season record and the top scorer. Below it:

- **Warmup Plan** - the team's warm-up plan
- **Rules** - the rules of the game and age-group formats
- **Coaching Materials**

The link to the Finnish FA's Taso service (lineups & results) is on the **Games** tab.

## Friendly matches

Mark a match as a **Friendly match** in the game form or later in **Match details**. Friendlies are kept out of competitive stats. In statistics, **Include friendly matches** brings them in.

## Archiving

When a league or tournament ends, archive it. Archived items are hidden from lists and pickers, but their games and statistics are kept. **Show archived** brings them back into view.
