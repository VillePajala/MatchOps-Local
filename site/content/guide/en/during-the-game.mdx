A match opens on the pitch. This guide covers the match view, the clock and recording goals.

## The match view

The view is stacked from top to bottom:

- **Score row** - teams and score
- **Player bar** - the match players as discs
- **Pitch** - the line-up on the pitch; bench players are shown as outlined discs
- **Control bar** - five buttons, with the clock in the middle

### Placing players

- **Place a player:** tap the player in the player bar, then tap the spot on the pitch.
- **Remove from the pitch:** double-tap a player on the pitch.
- **Goalkeeper:** tap the player in the player bar, then tap the shield icon that appears on the disc. Tap it again to remove the mark.
- **Formation:** the formation button on the control bar opens **Formations**; **Auto** picks one by player count. A formation places all selected players at once.

If the game was made from a plan, the starting line-up is already on the pitch.

### The control bar

Five buttons, left to right:

1. **Tactics Board** - opens the field tools and tactics mode (see [Tactics Board](/guide/tactics-board))
2. **Formations** - places the players in a preset formation
3. **Clock** (middle) - opens the large clock
4. **Reset Field** - clears the players, opponents and drawings from the pitch (the app asks you to confirm)
5. **Menu** - the match actions

### The match menu

**This match**: **Quick Save**, **Match details**, **Who is here?** (before kick-off only), **Record Performance** (when player assessments are on), **Finish this game** (progress, e.g. 1/2), **Team stats**, **Match planner** and **This game's plan** (when the game was made from a plan).

**Resources**: **Warmup Plan**, **Rules**, **Directions to the venue** (when the venue is known) and **Taso**.

At the bottom, **Settings** and **Home**. The phone's back button also returns to Home.

<Callout type="info">
Everything saves automatically after each action. **Quick Save** is there if you want to save right now.
</Callout>

## The large clock

Tap the clock on the control bar. The large clock shows the score, the time and the period at the top. Below them come the clock controls, the goal buttons **Goal for us** and **Opponent +1** right under **Start**, a compact **Next change** block (when the game has a plan) and, in the full layout, **Sub done**.

- **Start** / **Pause** / **Resume** - clock control
- **End period** / **End match** - moves the clock to the end of the period or the match if the game ends early
- When a period ends, the clock stops. The next period starts with **Start Period 2**.

<Callout type="info">
The first time the clock starts, the game is marked as played. A game that has not started (clock at zero, no events) shows on Home as an upcoming game.
</Callout>

## Goals

### Goal for us

<Steps>
<Step title="Goal for us">
Tap **Goal for us** on the large clock. The goal is logged and the score moves at once ("Goal logged · 12:34 · 2-1").
</Step>

<Step title="Who scored?">
Pick the scorer. The name buttons include every player in the match squad, not only the starters. **Skip** leaves the goal without a scorer - it still counts for the team.
</Step>

<Step title="Who assisted?">
Pick the assister or tap **Skip**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Goals can be corrected later in the goal log and in **Finish this game** (time, scorer, assister, delete).

### Opponent goal

Tap **Opponent +1** and confirm ("Add opponent goal?").

## Changes

### Next change

When the game has a plan, the large clock shows a **Next change** block: the time of the change (marked **at the break** if it happens between periods), who goes on (green arrow ↑) and who comes off (red arrow ↓). If earlier changes were missed, the block shows "+1 earlier". The block stays visible without confirming and moves on to the next change as time passes.

Changes are made on the touchline; the block tells you what the plan has next. The pitch's plan view switches with a button in the order **Lineup only** → **Planned substitutions** → **Substitutions and minutes**.

### Sub interval and Sub done

The sub interval shows on the large clock's info line (e.g. "sub 5 min"). Tap it to change the interval with the − and + buttons. The clock shows the time since the last change ("Since sub") and changes colour as the change time approaches and when it is due.

In the full layout, **Sub done** restarts the change timer after you have made the changes. The basic layout does not have this button.

<Callout type="info">
The change timer is a reminder. It does not record who was changed.
</Callout>

## End of the game

At full time the large clock shows **Finish this game**. If the game is level and goes to extra time or penalties, choose the link **Extra time or penalties**.

Continue in [Finish This Game](/guide/after-the-game).

## Voice notes

Hold the microphone button on the large clock (**Hold to dictate**) and say one observation at a time. Recordings stay on your phone. After the match you listen to them in **Finish this game**, write them out and attach each one to the right player or to the match. If you have connected your own AI provider, the app can also transcribe them for you.

## Fair Play Card

Menu → **Match details** → **Fair Play Card**: pick a player or clear the choice. A match can have one recipient.

## The screen stays on

MatchOps keeps the screen on for the whole match, including the breaks between periods.
