Practical tips to get the most out of MatchOps on match day.

## Before the game

### The day before

1. **Check the plan** - starters, keeper and changes: **Show rotation** on the Next match card (the plan is made in the **Match planner**)
2. **Share the plan** - **Share** on the Next match card sends it to an assistant coach or the team chat
3. **Mark known absences** - **Mark who is away** on the Next match card
4. **Charge your phone**

### At the ground

<Steps>
<Step title="Who is here?">
Tap **Mark who is away** on the Next match card and mark who is away. The plan is filled in with the players who are there.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the pitch">
**Open the game** opens the match on the pitch with the starting line-up in place.
</Step>
<Step title="Start the clock">
Open the large clock and tap **Start** at kick-off.
</Step>
</Steps>

## During the game

### Keep it simple

- **Goals** - **Goal for us** and **Opponent +1**. You can skip the scorer and add it later.
- **Changes** - follow the **Next change** block on the large clock
- **Observations** - dictate a voice note with the microphone button during a break in play

### Where to keep the phone

- Where you can glance at it without stopping coaching
- Where you can grab it quickly after a goal
- Protected from rain and knocks

### Weather

- **Cold** drains the battery fast - keep the phone in a pocket between uses
- **Rain** - use a protective case
- **Sun** - avoid direct sunlight on the screen

## After the game

### Right after the final whistle

1. **Finish this game** - check the score and goals
2. **Match sheet** - copy and paste where needed
3. **Match report** - a couple of sentences or dictate them, then **Share match report**

### Later

- Fill in the scorers if you skipped them during the game
- Record **Positions played** if you follow positions
- Do player assessments if they are on

## The player list

- Archive players who leave (statistics are kept)
- Add new players straight away
- Keep jersey numbers up to date so the **Match sheet** is ready to copy
- Use notes for things like a player's development goals. Notes are visible only to you.

## Fair Play Card

Many youth leagues give a Fair Play Card each match. Record it: match menu → **Match details** → **Fair Play Card** and pick the player. A match can have one recipient. The player's statistics show their **Fair Play Cards**.

## Leagues and tournaments

- Create the league or tournament before the first game so games link to it straight away
- Use short names without a year ("Spring League", "Helsinki Cup") - the club season is shown automatically
- Archive finished competitions to keep the lists tidy

## Keeping your data safe

Your data syncs to your account automatically. For extra safety:

- Save a backup file, for example at the end of a season (gear → **Backup & Restore**)
- Keep the backup somewhere other than the phone

## Rules

**Club** → **Rules** (and **Rules** in the match menu) shows the rules of the game and formats. It also links to the Finnish FA's Taso service, where you find your own league's regulations.

<Callout type="tip">
The best system is the one you keep using. Start with the plan, the goals and the match sheet - add more once that routine feels easy.
</Callout>
