A match day with MatchOps runs in four steps. Each has its own detailed guide.

<Steps>
<Step title="Plan ready">
The plan shows who starts where, who is in goal and when the changes happen. In the basic layout the app makes it when the game is created: check and edit it with **Show rotation** on Home and send it with **Share**. In the full layout you make the plan in the **Match planner** and prefill the game from it; then **Show rotation** and **Share** are on the Next match card on the **Games** tab. → [Before the Game](/guide/before-the-game)
</Step>

<Step title="Who is here?">
On match day mark who is away: **Start the match** on the basic-layout Home, or **Mark who is away** on the Next match card in the full layout. The plan is filled in with the players who are there, and the match opens on the pitch (straight away in the basic layout, with **Open the game** in the full layout). → [Before the Game](/guide/before-the-game)
</Step>

<Step title="Kick-off">
Open the large clock from the middle of the control bar and tap **Start**. Our goals: **Goal for us** → **Who scored?** → assister or **Skip**. Opponent goals: **Opponent +1**. The **Next change** block shows the plan's next change. → [During the Game](/guide/during-the-game)
</Step>

<Step title="Finish this game">
At full time: **Finish this game** → check the score and goals → **Match sheet** → **Match report** → **Share match report**. → [Finish This Game](/guide/after-the-game)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
Charge your phone the night before and check the plan at home. At the ground, all that is left is marking who is away.
</Callout>

In the full layout you can also attach a league or tournament and staff to the match (**Match details**) and plan several games at once in the Match planner. → [Full Layout: Recommended Setup](/guide/full-setup-path)
