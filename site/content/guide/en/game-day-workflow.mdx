A match day with MatchOps runs in four steps. Each has its own detailed guide.

<Steps>
<Step title="Plan ready">
The plan shows who starts where, who is in goal and when the changes happen. In the basic layout the plan is made when you add a game (**New game** → **Make the plan**). In the full layout you make it in the **Match planner** - for one game or a whole tournament day - and prefill the game from it. Then **Show rotation** and **Share** are on the Next game card on Home (in the full layout, the Next match card on the **Games** tab). → [Before the Game](/guide/before-the-game)
</Step>

<Step title="Who is here?">
On match day mark who is away. In the basic layout, **Start the match** on the card first asks who is here and then opens the match on the pitch. In the full layout, use **Mark who is away** on the Next match card and open the match with **Open the game**. The plan is filled in with the players who are there. → [Before the Game](/guide/before-the-game)
</Step>

<Step title="Kick-off">
Open the large clock from the middle of the control bar and tap **Start**. Our goals: **Goal for us** → **Who scored?** → assister or **Skip**. Opponent goals: **Opponent +1**. The **Next change** block shows the plan's next change. → [During the Game](/guide/during-the-game)
</Step>

<Step title="Finish this game">
At full time: **Finish this game** → check the score and goals → **Match sheet** → **Match report** → **Share match report**. → [Finish This Game](/guide/after-the-game)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
Charge your phone the night before and check the plan at home. At the ground, all that is left is marking who is away.
</Callout>

You can also attach a league or tournament and staff to the match (**Match details**). → [Recommended Setup](/guide/full-setup-path)
