On the tactics board you draw plays, set pieces and movement and show them to the players, for example at half-time.

## Opening it

Tap the first button on the control bar, **Tactics Board**. The pitch turns into the tactics board, drawing is ready straight away, and the control bar shows the board's tools.

The tactics board is separate from the match line-up: what you put on the board does not change the match pitch. The cross (**Back**) closes the board and brings back the match pitch.

## Tools

The control bar buttons, left to right:

- **Back** - closes the tactics board
- **Undo** / **Redo** - the tactics board has its own history
- **Add Home Disc** - a disc for your own team
- **Add Opponent Disc** - an opponent disc
- **Clear Drawings** - removes the drawings
- **Reset Field** - clears the tactics board: discs, drawings and the ball position (the app asks you to confirm)

## Drawing, discs and the ball

Draw with your finger straight onto the pitch - lines, arrows and runs. Discs and the ball can be moved by dragging them. Use the ball to show where a situation starts.

## Ways to use it

- **At half-time** - show what changes in the second half
- **Set pieces** - corners, free kicks, throw-ins and kick-offs
- **Movement** - runs, passing sequences and defensive shifts
- **Before the game** - go through the game's themes

## Pitch type

The pitch is drawn for the match's sport: **Football** (penalty and goal areas, centre circle) or **Futsal** (a smaller court with curved penalty areas). The sport is chosen during setup or in the game form.
