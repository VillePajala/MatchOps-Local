On the tactics board you draw plays, set pieces and movement and show them to the players, for example at half-time. The board is available in both layouts.

## Opening it

Tap the first button on the control bar, **Tactics Board**. **Field Tools** opens, tactics mode turns on and drawing is ready straight away. The **Tactics** button at the top switches between tactics mode and the normal pitch.

Tactics mode is separate from the match line-up: what you put on the board does not change the match pitch.

## Drawing

Draw with your finger straight onto the pitch - lines, arrows and runs.

- **Undo** / **Redo** - the tactics board has its own history
- **Clear** - removes the drawings
- **Reset** - clears the tactics board: discs, drawings and the ball position

## Discs and the ball

- **Add Home** - a disc for your own team
- **Add Opp** - an opponent disc
- The tactics board also has a ball to show where a situation starts.

## The normal pitch

With **Tactics** off, the field tools hold the match pitch actions:

- **Place All** - places all selected players on the pitch
- **Add Opp** - adds an opponent player to the pitch
- **Clear** - removes drawings from the match pitch
- **Reset** - resets the pitch

Placing a player on the match pitch always works the same way: tap the player in the player bar, then tap the spot on the pitch.

## Ways to use it

- **At half-time** - show what changes in the second half
- **Set pieces** - corners, free kicks, throw-ins and kick-offs
- **Movement** - runs, passing sequences and defensive shifts
- **Before the game** - go through the game's themes

## Pitch type

The pitch is drawn for the match's sport: **Football** (penalty and goal areas, centre circle) or **Futsal** (a smaller court with curved penalty areas). The sport is chosen during setup or in the game form.
