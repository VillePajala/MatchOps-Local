Your data is saved to your free account and on your phone at the same time. The app also works without a connection, and changes sync in the background when you are back online.

## How data is stored

- **Account** - free, email and password
- **Sync** - the same data on every device you sign in on
- **Safe in the cloud** - your data stays even if you change phones
- **Offline** - changes save on the phone at once and sync later

Data is stored in Supabase in the EU (Stockholm). Row-level security makes sure only you can see your data. It is never sold or used for advertising.

## Account

### Creating an account

1. Open the app and choose **Create a free account**
2. Enter your email and a password (at least 8 characters) and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
3. Type the code from your email at **Enter Verification Code** and tap **Verify**

### Signing in

Choose **Sign in**, enter your email and password and tap **Sign In**.

### Forgotten password

1. Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen
2. Enter your email and tap **Send Reset Email**
3. Type the code from your email at **Enter Reset Code**
4. **Set New Password**

### Signing out

Gear → **Sign out**, or **Settings** → **Account** → **Sign Out**.

<Callout type="warning">
Before signing out, make sure the sync status shows no pending changes. If there are some, the app warns you before signing out.
</Callout>

## Sync status

The sync status shows in the app:

- **Synced** - all data is in the cloud
- **Syncing** - upload in progress
- **Pending** - changes waiting to sync
- **Offline** - no connection; changes sync when you are online
- **Error** - tap to retry

## Settings

Gear → **Settings**. Tabs:

- **General** - **Language**, **Layout** (see [Quick Start](/guide/getting-started)), **Default Team Name**, **Getting to matches**, **Season Start Date**
- **Data** - backup and restore, **Download Your Data**, **Privacy** (including **Error reporting and usage statistics** and **Notice to players' families**) and the **Danger Zone**
- **Account** - the cloud account, **Email Preferences** and signing out
- **About** - **App Version**, **Storage Usage**, **Check for Updates** and **Send Feedback**

## Backups

On top of sync, you can save a backup to a file.

- Gear → **Backup & Restore**, or
- **Settings** → **Data** → **Backup & Export** → **Backup All Data**

The backup is a JSON file with players, matches, teams, leagues, tournaments, staff and settings. Keep it somewhere safe, such as a cloud folder.

The app also makes **Automatic Restore Points**, listed in the same place.

## Restore

1. **Settings** → **Data** → **Restore from Backup** (or gear → **Backup & Restore**)
2. Pick the backup file
3. Confirm: restoring replaces all current data

<Callout type="warning">
A restore cannot be undone. Back up your current data first.
</Callout>

### A new phone

Install MatchOps and sign in with the same account. Your data syncs automatically.

## Excel export

- A match: **Finish this game** → **Export Excel**
- Combined statistics: the export button in the statistics view (**Export Excel**)

## Deleting data

- **A single match** - **All games** (in the Full view **Games** → **Saved games**) and the match's **Delete** action.
- **Re-sync from Cloud** - clears the phone's copy and downloads the data again from your account (Settings → **Data** → Danger Zone)
- **Delete All Data** - permanently deletes the data from the phone and the cloud. Your account stays.
- **Delete Account** - permanently deletes the account and all its data. **Export backup first** if you want to keep the data.

<Callout type="tip">
Take a backup before deleting anything.
</Callout>
