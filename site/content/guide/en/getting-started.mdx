This guide takes you from installing the app to your first ready-made game plan in a few minutes. MatchOps works for both football and futsal.

## Install

MatchOps is available only for Android phones, on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.matchops.local). Install it from there and open it.

## 1. Create a free account

The first time you open the app you see **"Plan, rotate, record."** and the main points of the app. Choose **Create a free account** (or **Sign in** if you already have one).

<Steps>
<Step title="Email and password">
Enter your email and a password (at least 8 characters). The eye icon shows the password. Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
</Step>

<Step title="Verification code">
The app emails you a verification code. Type it in at **Enter Verification Code** and tap **Verify**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
With an account your data stays safe, even if you change phones. Once signed in, the app also works without a connection - changes sync when you are back online.
</Callout>

## 2. Your team (Step 1 / 2)

A new account opens a short two-step setup.

- **Team name** - e.g. "FC Honka P12"
- **Sport** - **Football** or **Futsal**
- **Age group (optional)** - the app suggests a format from the age group
- **Format** - e.g. 5v5 or 8v8. The suggestion reads "Suggested for the age group: ... You can pick any."

Tap **Continue**.

## 3. Players (Step 2 / 2)

Paste a list of names or type one at a time:

- **Paste from clipboard** adds the whole list at once, one name per line. Numbers at the start of a line (such as "7." or "#12") are removed, so you can copy the list straight from the team chat.
- First names are enough. Numbers can be added later.

Tap **Done**. To do this later, choose **Skip, I'll do this later**.

## 4. When is the next game?

Next the app asks one thing: **When is the next game?** "We'll make a ready plan for the game. You can change everything later."

- **Date** - pick a day (or **Today**)
- **Against whom?** (optional) - left empty, the opponent is saved as "Opponent". You can add the name later.

Tap **Make the plan**. Only now are the game and its plan created. **Not now - go to Home** goes straight to Home.

## 5. The plan

The **Plan** screen shows the game ready to go: who starts where, who is in goal and when the changes happen. The top line shows the player count, format and length (e.g. "10 players · 5v5 · 2 × 15 min").

- **Swap two players** - tap one and then the other.
- **Keeper** - the line "In goal: Onni · **change**" opens **Who goes in goal?**
- **change** - next to the date and the length (periods and minutes per period).
- **Shuffle again** - makes a new suggestion.
- **Planned playing time** - how long each player is on the pitch in the plan.
- **Detailed editing** - opens the same game in the full **Match planner**. When you close it, you are back on this screen with the changes.

When the plan is ready, tap **Looks good**. You land on Home.

<Callout type="tip">
The plan does not lock anything. Change it any time before the game from **Show rotation** on Home. More: [Before the Game: the Plan and Who Is Here?](/guide/before-the-game)
</Callout>

## 6. Home (basic layout)

New accounts start in the **basic layout**. Home has no tabs, only what matters on match day:

- The **Next game** card: date, opponent and "Rotation ready · 14 players"
  - **Start the match** (on match day) or **Mark who is away** (other days) - both first open **Who is here?**
  - **Show rotation** - opens the plan for editing
  - **Share** - sends the plan as text, for example to the team chat
- **Team · 14 players** - opens the player list: edit name, number, nickname and goalkeeper mark, add or remove players
- **All games** - every game, once the first one exists
- **Statistics** - once the first game has been played
- **New game** - asks "When is the next game?" again and makes a plan for the new game. **More settings** opens the full game form.
- **All features** - shows what the full layout adds

If a match is under way, the card shows it first so you can continue. If a game was not started and its date has passed, the card offers **Move to another day** and **Remove**.

## The basic and full layouts

MatchOps has two layouts. Both use the same data - switching never removes anything.

**Basic layout** (new accounts):

- Home has the Next game card, Team, All games, Statistics and New game - no tabs
- **New game** asks "When is the next game?" and makes a ready plan
- Plans are made one game at a time; **Detailed editing** opens the full Match planner
- The large clock has no **Sub done** button
- The extra items of **Finish this game** are folded under **More records**

**Full layout**:

- Home has the tabs **Games**, **Club**, **Competitions** and **Stats**
- **New game** opens the full **Create New Match** form
- The **Match planner** for several games, such as a tournament day
- Leagues and tournaments, staff, warm-up plan and rules, venues and departure times, games played elsewhere in player statistics
- **Sub done** on the large clock
- **Finish this game** shows every item directly

The match view, quick goal entry, **Who is here?** and **Finish this game** work the same in both layouts.

**Switching:**

- From the basic layout: **All features** → **Switch to the full layout**
- From either: gear (top right) → **Settings** → **Layout** → **Basic** or **Full**

<Callout type="info">
Accounts created before this update stay in the full layout. Setting up the full layout is covered in [Recommended Setup](/guide/full-setup-path).
</Callout>
