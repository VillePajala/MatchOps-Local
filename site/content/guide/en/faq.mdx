## Getting started

### How do I install the app?

MatchOps is available only for Android phones, on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.matchops.local). There is no iPhone app.

### Do I need an account?

Yes. The account is free: an email, a password and a verification code sent to your email. With an account your data stays safe, even if you change phones.

### Does MatchOps cost anything?

No. The account and the app are free.

## The plan and absences

### What is the plan?

A game's plan shows who starts where, who is in goal and when the changes happen. In the basic layout the plan is made when you add a game (**New game** → **Make the plan**). In the full layout you make the plan in the **Match planner** (for one game or several at once, such as a tournament day) and prefill a new game from it. See [Before the Game](/guide/before-the-game).

### How do I mark who is away?

From the Next game card on Home (in the full layout, the Next match card on the **Games** tab): **Mark who is away**. **Who is here?** opens - tap the players who are away and save. Before kick-off you also find it in the match menu.

### Does the whole plan change when someone is away?

No. Your changes are kept, and only the absent player's spots and changes are filled with players who are there. If you had moved players on the pitch by hand, the app warns before the line-up is made again.

### Does the game count as played for an absent player?

No. An absent player is not in the match squad, so the game does not show in their statistics.

### Why can't I mark absences any more?

Absences can only be marked before kick-off. Once the clock has started or the match has events, change the squad in **Match details**.

### How do I share the plan?

When the next game has a plan, **Share** on the Next game card on Home (in the full layout, the Next match card) sends it as text, for example to the team chat. **Show rotation** on the same card opens the plan.

## The match

### How do I record a goal quickly?

On the large clock, **Goal for us**. The score moves at once; then pick **Who scored?** and the assister, or tap **Skip**.

### Can I edit a match afterwards?

Yes. Open the game (in the basic layout **All games**, in the full layout **Games** → **Saved games**) and choose **Finish this game** from the match menu. There you can edit goals, the squad, the match sheet and the match report.

### What is the difference between the match sheet and the match report?

The **Match sheet** has every detail (squad, score, goals) you need to fill in the official match sheet in Taso. The **Match report** is your own text about the match; **Share match report** sends it with the score, scorers and line-up.

## Cloud and sync

### Can I use the app on several devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your data syncs.

### Does the app work without a connection?

Yes, once you have signed in. Changes save on the phone at once and sync when the connection is back, so you can track a whole match offline. Signing in needs a connection.

### I used the app before creating an account. Can I move that data to my account?

Yes. **Settings** → **Account** → **Import Local Data to Cloud**.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Data is stored in Supabase in the EU (Stockholm), and only you can access it. It is never sold or used for advertising. You can delete your account and all data at any time: **Settings** → **Data**.

## Data management

### How do I back up my data?

Gear → **Backup & Restore**, or **Settings** → **Data** → **Backup All Data**.

### What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on the new device with the same account - your data is there.

### Can I manage several teams?

Yes: **Club** → **Teams**.

### Can I share statistics?

Yes. Export statistics as an Excel file and send the file. Match data can also be shared with the **Match sheet** and **Share match report**.

## Technical questions

### How does the app update?

The app updates automatically when you open it online. When a new version is ready, you see **Update Available**. Tap **Install Update** and then **Reload Now**.

### How much space does the app use?

Over 100 games with statistics typically take less than 50 MB.
