## Getting started

### How do I install the app?

On Android, install MatchOps from Google Play. In a browser, open [match-ops.com](https://match-ops.com) and choose the browser's "Add to Home screen".

### Do I need an account?

Yes. The account is free: an email, a password and a verification code sent to your email. With an account your data stays safe, even if you change phones.

### Does MatchOps cost anything?

No. The account and the app are free.

### What does the setup ask?

The team name, sport, age group and format, then the players' names (you can paste the whole list). Finally the app asks **When is the next game?** and makes a ready plan for the game. See [Quick Start](/guide/getting-started).

## The basic and full layouts

### Why are there no tabs on Home?

You are in the **basic layout**, which shows what matters on match day: the next game, the team, the games and statistics. The **Games**, **Club**, **Competitions** and **Stats** tabs are in the **full layout**.

### How do I switch to the full layout?

From the basic-layout Home, **All features** → **Switch to the full layout**, or gear → **Settings** → **Layout** → **Full**. Nothing is removed, and you can switch back in the same setting.

### What does the full layout add?

The Home tabs, the Match planner for several games (e.g. a tournament day), leagues and tournaments, staff, warm-up plan and rules, venues and departure times, and adding games played elsewhere to player statistics. The large clock also has **Reset sub timer**.

### Why can't I find Reset sub timer?

It is only in the full layout. In the basic layout the large clock shows the plan's next change without a separate change-timer reset.

## The plan and absences

### What is the plan?

A game's plan shows who starts where, who is in goal and when the changes happen. In the basic layout the app makes it when you create the game. In the full layout you make the plan in the **Match planner** (for one game or several at once) and prefill the game from it. In the basic-layout plan you can swap players, change the keeper, shuffle again or edit in detail. See [Before the Game](/guide/before-the-game).

### How do I mark who is away?

From the next game's card on Home: **Start the match** (basic layout, on match day) or **Mark who is away**. **Who is here?** opens - tap the players who are away and save. Before kick-off you also find it in the match menu.

### Does the whole plan change when someone is away?

No. Your changes are kept, and only the absent player's spots and changes are filled with players who are there. If you had moved players on the pitch by hand, the app warns before the line-up is made again.

### Does the game count as played for an absent player?

No. An absent player is not in the match squad, so the game does not show in their statistics.

### Why can't I mark absences any more?

Absences can only be marked before kick-off. Once the clock has started or the match has events, change the squad in **Match details**.

### How do I share the plan?

In the basic layout, **Share** on the Next game card sends the plan as text, for example to the team chat. The full-layout Match planner has no Share.

## The match

### How do I record a goal quickly?

On the large clock, **Goal for us**. The score moves at once; then pick **Who scored?** and the assister, or tap **Skip**.

### Can I edit a match afterwards?

Yes. Open the game (**All games** or **Saved games**) and choose **Finish this game** from the match menu. There you can edit goals, the squad, the match sheet and the match report.

### What is the difference between the match sheet and the match report?

The **Match sheet** is the match data (squad, score, goals) ready to copy. The **Match report** is your own text about the match; **Share match report** sends it with the score, scorers and line-up.

## Cloud and sync

### Can I use the app on several devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your data syncs.

### Does the app work without a connection?

Yes. Changes save on the phone at once and sync when the connection is back. You can track a whole match offline.

### I used the app before creating an account. Can I move that data to my account?

Yes. **Settings** → **Account** → **Import Local Data to Cloud**.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Data is stored in Supabase in the EU region, and only you can access it. It is not shared with third parties. You can delete your account and all data at any time.

## Data management

### How do I back up my data?

Gear → **Backup & Restore**, or **Settings** → **Data** → **Backup All Data**.

### What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on the new device with the same account - your data is there.

### Can I manage several teams?

Yes, in the full layout (**Club** → **Teams**).

### Can I share statistics?

Yes. Export statistics as an Excel file and send the file. Match data can also be shared with the **Match sheet** and **Share match report**.

## Technical questions

### How does the app update?

The app updates automatically when you open it online. You get a notice about the new version.

### How much space does the app use?

Over 100 games with statistics typically take less than 50 MB.
