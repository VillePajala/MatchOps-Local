The player list is the foundation of MatchOps. The same players appear in plans, matches and statistics.

## Opening the player list

In the basic layout, the **Team** row on Home opens **All Players**. In the full layout, **Players** on the **Club** tab opens it.

## Adding players

<Steps>
<Step title="Add Player">
Tap **Add Player** in the list.
</Step>
<Step title="Details">
- **Player Name** (required)
- **Nickname** - shown on the disc on the pitch if given
- **Jersey Number** (optional, must be unique)
- **Notes** (optional)
- **Goalkeeper** - marks the player as a goalkeeper
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
The player is added to the list.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Editing

Tap a player in the list. **Player Details** opens, and you can change the name, nickname, number, notes and goalkeeper mark.

Jersey numbers must be unique. To swap two players' numbers, first give one of them a temporary number.

## Row actions

The player row's **Actions** menu:

- **Stats** - opens the player's statistics
- **Add to stats** - adds a game the player played elsewhere (see [Statistics](/guide/statistics))
- **Archive** - hides the player from the list and pickers; statistics are kept. **Show archived players** lists them, and **Restore** brings a player back.
- **Delete** - removes the player from the club roster and every team. Played matches keep the player's statistics.

<Callout type="tip">
When a player leaves mid-season, **Archive** is usually better than **Delete**.
</Callout>

## Search and sorting

The search field (**Search players...**) finds by name. **Sort by**: **Last Name**, **Newest** or **Oldest**.

## Several teams

The same players can be shared across several teams (**Club** → **Teams**). See [Teams & Organization](/guide/teams-organization).
