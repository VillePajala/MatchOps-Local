The player list is the foundation of MatchOps. The same players appear in plans, matches and statistics.

## Opening the player list

- **Basic layout:** the **Team · 14 players** row on Home
- **Full layout:** **Club** → **Players**

Both open **All Players**.

## Adding players

### During setup or as a list

During setup (and in the basic layout's **Add players** card when there are no players yet) you can paste a whole list of names: **Paste from clipboard** or type the names, one per line. Numbers and bullets at the start of a line are removed. First names are enough.

### One at a time

<Steps>
<Step title="Add Player">
Tap **Add Player** in the list.
</Step>
<Step title="Details">
- **Player Name** (required)
- **Nickname** - shown on the disc on the pitch if given
- **Jersey Number** (optional, must be unique)
- **Notes** (optional)
- **Goalkeeper** - marks the player as a goalkeeper
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
The player is added to the list.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Editing

Tap a player in the list. **Player Details** opens, and you can change the name, nickname, number, notes and goalkeeper mark.

Jersey numbers must be unique. To swap two players' numbers, first give one of them a temporary number.

## Row actions

The player row's **Actions** menu:

- **Stats** - opens the player's statistics
- **Add to stats** - adds a game the player played elsewhere (see [Statistics](/guide/statistics))
- **Archive** - hides the player from the list and pickers; statistics are kept. **Show archived players** lists them, and **Restore** brings a player back.
- **Delete** - removes the player from the club roster and every team. Played matches keep the player's statistics.

<Callout type="tip">
When a player leaves mid-season, **Archive** is usually better than **Delete**.
</Callout>

## Search and sorting

The search field (**Search players...**) finds by name. **Sort by**: **Last Name**, **Newest** or **Oldest**.

## Player assessments

Player assessments are off by default. Turn them on: gear → **Settings** → **Player assessments**. In the same place you choose:

- **Assessment metrics**: **Balanced (10)**, **Light 6 (U7-U9)** or **Creative-attacking (14)**
- **Assessment rating style**: **Words (5 levels)**, **Numbers 1-5** or **Numbers 1-10**

Switching is safe - past ratings are kept.

### Metrics (Balanced)

- **Ball control** - first touch and keeping the ball under pressure
- **Passing** - weight, timing and choice of pass
- **Scanning** - looks around and gathers information before receiving
- **Game understanding** - positioning and reading the game, both attacking and defending
- **Decision-making** - makes the right choice for the moment
- **Courage** - demands the ball, plays forward, dares to take risks
- **Effort** - work rate, tracks back, keeps trying
- **Enjoyment** - visible joy and engagement in playing
- **Teamwork** - shares the ball and supports team-mates
- **Fair play** - respect for the referee, opponents, own mistakes and defeat

**Light 6** uses Ball control, Courage, Effort, Enjoyment, Teamwork and Fair play. **Creative-attacking** adds Creativity, Dribbling, Finishing and Off-ball movement to Balanced.

### Assessing after a match

- Match menu → **Record Performance**, or
- **Finish this game** → **Player assessments** → **Open player assessments** (in the basic layout under **More records**)

For each player you can give per-metric ratings, an **Overall** rating and notes. The results show in the player's statistics: **Performance Ratings**, **Rating Trends**, **Strengths** and **Focus areas**, plus an exportable **Development report**.

<Callout type="info">
Assessments and notes are yours alone. They are not shared with anyone.
</Callout>

## Several teams

In the full layout the same players can be shared across several teams (**Club** → **Teams**). See [Teams & Organization](/guide/teams-organization).
