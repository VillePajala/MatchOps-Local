A game's plan shows who starts where, who is in goal and when the changes happen. How the plan is made depends on the layout:

- **Basic layout** (new accounts): the app makes a one-game plan when you create the game. See **The one-game plan (basic layout)** below.
- **Full layout**: plans are made in the **Match planner** - for one game or several at once, such as a tournament day - and a game is prefilled from the plan. Creating a game does not make a plan on its own. See **The Match planner (full layout)** below.

On match day you mark who is away in both layouts, and the plan is filled in with the players who are there.

## The one-game plan (basic layout)

In the basic layout the plan is made when you create a game with **When is the next game?** and tap **Make the plan**. That happens after the first setup and every time you choose **New game** on the basic-layout Home.

The plan uses the team's format and players. Every selected player gets playing time in the plan; the coach decides the final split.

### Editing the plan

Open the plan from the Next game card on the basic-layout Home: **Show rotation**.

- **Swap two players:** tap one and then the other. This works for the keeper too.
- **Keeper:** "In goal: Onni · **change**" → **Who goes in goal?**
- **Date and length:** tap **change** next to the date or the length (1 or 2 periods, minutes per period).
- **Shuffle again:** a new suggestion from the same players.
- **Changes:** the line "Changes:" shows the change times and how many players change each time.
- **Planned playing time:** each player's planned minutes.
- **Detailed editing:** opens the same game in the full Match planner (positions, change minutes, balance). When you close it, you are back in the plan with the changes.

When the plan is ready: **Looks good**.

<Callout type="warning">
If you have already moved players by hand on the game's pitch, the app asks first (**Moved by hand**). **Use the plan** replaces the hand-made changes with the plan.
</Callout>

### Sharing the plan

**Share** on the basic-layout Next game card sends the plan as text through your phone's share menu - for example to an assistant coach or the team chat. The text has the match, the starters (keeper marked), who comes on and goes off at which minute, and who is away.

If the phone cannot share, the text is copied to the clipboard ("Rotation copied - paste it into the chat.").

## Who is here?

On match day you mark absences on one screen. It is the same in both layouts.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open it">
The basic-layout Home's Next game card: **Start the match** on match day or **Mark who is away** on other days. The Next match card on the full-layout **Games** tab: **Mark who is away**. In an opened match before kick-off: the match menu → **Who is here?**
</Step>

<Step title="Tap the absent players">
Everyone starts ticked. Tap the players who are away; the top shows how many are here (e.g. "12 / 14").
</Step>

<Step title="Opponent">
If the opponent is still saved as "Opponent", the screen also asks for the opponent's name.
</Step>

<Step title="Save or start">
**Save** (other days) or **Start** (match day - the match opens on the pitch).
</Step>
</Steps>

### What an absence changes

- **A game with a plan:** the absent player is marked absent for this game in the plan. Your changes to the plan (keeper, swaps, positions, change times) are kept; only the absent player's spots and changes are filled with players who are there. The line-up and the planned changes are applied to the game.
- **A game without a plan:** the game's squad becomes the players who are here, and absent players leave the pitch.
- **Statistics:** an absent player is not in the match squad, so the game does not count as played for them.

<Callout type="warning">
If you have moved players on the pitch by hand, the screen warns before saving: "The line-up will be made again from the plan. Changes made by hand on the pitch are replaced."
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Absences can only be marked before kick-off. Once the clock has started or the match has events, the screen says: "The match has already started, so absences can no longer be marked." During the game, change the squad in **Match details**.
</Callout>

## The Match planner (full layout)

In the full layout plans are made in the **Match planner** - for one game or several at once, such as a tournament day. In the full layout it is on Home's **Games** tab, and in both layouts in the match menu. From the basic-layout Home, the full planner for one game opens from the plan's **Detailed editing** link.

<Steps>
<Step title="New plan">
**Plans** → **New plan**. Give a name (e.g. "Sunday tournament"), pick a team (optional), the players, the number of games, the formation, periods and minutes per period. Tap **Create plan**.
</Step>

<Step title="Suggest lineups">
On the **Games** tab, **Suggest lineups** builds starting line-ups and changes for every included game. Edit each game on its own (**Edit lineup**).
</Step>

<Step title="Absences per game">
In a game's line-up, a player can be marked absent for that game (**Absent from this game**).
</Step>

<Step title="Check the balance">
The **Balance** tab shows minutes and positions per player across all games of the plan.
</Step>

<Step title="Create games from the plan">
In the full-layout game form (**Create New Match**), choose **Prefill from plan (optional)** and the plan's game. The line-up and planned changes come with it. When you change the plan later, **Update ... games created from this** updates the unplayed games; played games are never changed.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
Plans can be duplicated, archived, exported to a file and imported from a file in the plan's actions.
</Callout>
