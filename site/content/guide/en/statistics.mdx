Statistics build up automatically from your matches: results, goals, assists, games played, positions and (if on) player assessments.

## Opening statistics

- **Home, basic layout:** the **Statistics** row (shown once the first game has been played)
- **Home, full layout:** the **Stats** tab. At the top are wins-draws-losses (W–D–L), goal difference and the top scorers; below them **League stats**, **Tournament stats**, **Overall stats**, **Player stats** and **Add to player stats**
- **During a match:** menu → **Team stats**
- **From the player list:** the player's actions → **Stats**

## Tabs

- **Current** - the open match (this is the **Finish this game** screen, see [Finish This Game](/guide/after-the-game))
- **League** - league games combined
- **Tournament** - tournament games combined
- **Overall** - all games
- **Player** - one player's statistics

## Filters

On the league and tournament tabs, first pick a league or tournament (or **All Leagues** / **All Tournaments**). In the **Filters** panel:

- **Team**
- Sport (**All Sports**, Football, Futsal)
- **Gender**
- Club season (e.g. "24/25")
- **Level** - for a tournament with competition levels

Friendly matches are left out unless **Include friendly matches** is chosen.

## Team numbers

The league, tournament and overall views show games, wins-draws-losses, goals and goal difference.

- **By opponent** - your record against each opponent
- If the same team is spelled in several ways, the app says so; **Check team names** merges the spellings
- **Position balance** - who has played in which line (GK, DEF, MID, ATT) or position, based on recorded actual positions

## Player table

Columns: player, games, goals, assists and points. Tap a column header to sort. Tap a player row to open their statistics on the **Player** tab. The bottom row is **Totals**.

## Player tab

Search for a player (**Search players...**). You see:

- **Games Played**, **Goals**, **Assists**, **Points** and per-game averages
- **Positions played** - which lines the player has played in
- **Fair Play Cards**, captain games and **Player of Tournament** awards
- **League Performance** and **Tournament Performance**
- **Game Log** - games one by one; tap a game to open it
- With player assessments on: **Performance Ratings**, **Rating Trends**, **Strengths**, **Focus areas**, the **By difficulty** and **Current form** options and **Export development report**

### Add to player stats

A game that was not recorded in the app (e.g. a game for another team) can be added to a player's statistics:

- **Stats** → **Add to player stats**, or the player's actions → **Add to stats**
- Details: league or tournament (optional), home/away/neutral, team and opponent, score, date, games, goals and assists, positions and a note
- **Include in league/tournament statistics** - choose this if the game was played for the same team

Added games are marked **ADDED** in the statistics.

## Excel export

- A single match: **Finish this game** → **Export Excel**
- Combined statistics: the export button in the statistics view (**Export Excel**)

## Club season

The club season decides which season (e.g. "24/25") a game belongs to. Set the start date in gear → **Settings** → **General** → **Season Start Date**.
