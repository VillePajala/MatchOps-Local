Tactics board, stopwatch, notebook and statistics - in one app.

MatchOps replaces a coach's scattered tools with a single app. Plan line-ups and playing time in advance, follow and record matches as they happen, and share a finished summary afterwards. Comprehensive statistics build up automatically.

PLAN (a single match)
• Place players on an interactive pitch in the formation you want
• Build a line-up quickly with ready-made formations
• Draw tactics and set plays on the tactics board

PLAYING-TIME PLANNER (a set of matches)
• Build line-ups for several matches at once, a whole tournament for example
• The balance view shows minutes and positions per player across the whole set
• Playing time is colour-coded per player, so you spot an uneven split at a glance
• The app flags anyone left without minutes, sitting out a whole match, or playing only one position throughout
• Create the matches straight from the plan, and apply the plan when the match starts
• During the match the app reminds you of the substitutions you planned

RECORD
• Follow the match clock and substitution intervals
• Log goals and assists
• Log extra time and penalty shoot-outs
• Draw tactics at half-time
• The match log builds itself
• Dictate a note during the match with one press

AFTER THE MATCH
• Record the positions each player actually played
• Write the match report and share a finished summary
• Finish the match step by step: line-up, league and team, goals and scorers, positions played, notes on players, and finally the summary to share
• A checklist shows what is missing and takes you straight to the fix: it points out, for example, if the goals logged do not match the score, or a goal has no scorer
• Copy the squad and match report already laid out for Palloliitto's Taso system: shirt numbers, goalkeeper, final score, half-time score and every goal with its minute
• Write your voice notes up and attach them to the right player or to the match

DEVELOP
• Player, tournament, league, season and team statistics build up automatically
• Share a player summary: matches, positions, goals and assists, and the notes you recorded
• Follow how positions are shared across the team, and each player's matches across seasons and tournaments

RULES
• Browse or search the laws of the game and open the official rulebook straight at the right page
• A link to Palloliitto's results service, where you can check the rules and regulations for your age group and league

MANAGE
• Create and manage several teams and squads
• Organise leagues and tournaments
• Browse your match history
• Keep track of team staff

CLOUD AND BACKUPS
• Sign in and sync your data across devices
• Your data is stored securely in the cloud
• Back up and restore with files as well
• Export statistics to Excel

FOOTBALL AND FUTSAL
• Separate pitch views for football and futsal
• Every match tagged by sport for statistics and filtering
• Available in Finnish and English
• Formats from 3v3 to 11v11

Who is it for? Football and futsal coaches at every level - from junior teams (U7-U21) to recreational and competitive sides - who want a simple way to run their matches and support their players' development.
